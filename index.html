About Me

I am a board-certified psychiatrist providing comprehensive outpatient and telehealth services for adults. My approach combines careful diagnostic evaluation, evidence-based medication management, and a collaborative treatment plan tailored to each individual.

My Approach

I believe effective psychiatric care begins with listening. Every patient's experience is unique, and treatment should reflect that. My goal is to create a space where you feel heard, respected, and actively involved in your care.

Treatment Focus

I treat a range of conditions including anxiety disorders, depression, bipolar disorder, ADHD, and trauma-related disorders. Whether you are navigating ongoing symptoms or a new life transition, I aim to provide clear guidance and structured, compassionate care.
